FAQ

Questions?
We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before your hospital starts using CareWell HMS.

Talk to our team →
Can the system support multiple departments? +

Yes. Departments, doctors, schedules, services and permissions can be configured independently while sharing a unified patient record.

Can different staff have different access? +

Yes. The HMS is designed around role-based access so reception, doctors, nurses, pharmacy, lab and administrators can see the information relevant to their work.

Can we manage appointments and queues? +

Yes. Appointment slots, doctor availability, queues, rescheduling and follow-up workflows are part of the platform design.

Can billing, pharmacy and laboratory be connected? +

Yes. These modules are designed to share patient and encounter context while keeping each workflow separated and auditable.
